# Hyperframes Composition Brief: MUJINA

## Objective
Create a short, electrifying launch-style brag video for MUJINA (Open-Source Bitcoin Mining Firmware).

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080
- Duration: 20 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: `d:\github\mujina`
- Primary files read: `README.md`, `dashboard.html`, `mujina-architecture.visual-check.2048x1320.dark.png`
- Product name: MUJINA
- Tagline / strongest claim: "You bought the hardware, but someone else controls the software. Zero dev fee. Own your firmware."
- Key UI or visual moment to recreate: Cyberpunk Fleet Monitor HUD, polygonal metric cards (Hashrate, Efficiency J/TH, Stratum connection), and system architecture schematic.

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Industrial cyberpunk mining revolution
- Interpretation: Obsidian grid background, Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) and Hot Magenta (`#FF2E97`) glows, polygonal borders, and high-impact audio sync.
- Hook: "YOU BOUGHT THE HARDWARE. WHY DOES SOMEONE ELSE CONTROL IT?"
- Outro / punchline: "MUJINA // OWN YOUR FIRMWARE. Run your hashboards without permission."
