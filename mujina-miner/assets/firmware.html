One-step, no confirmation. Uploading a bundle here swaps in whatever it contains and reboots the device immediately — there's no separate "apply" click. The upload is checked for a valid ELF header (and, for mujina-minerd, the nano3s feature symbols) before anything is written, but a working build that's simply wrong for this hardware can still be uploaded. Mining stops for a couple of minutes while it reboots.
