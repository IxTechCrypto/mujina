**Post Copy:**

Did you know that almost every Bitcoin mining machine in the world runs closed, proprietary firmware?

You keep every single satoshi your machines hash.

Whether you're experimenting at home with a Bitaxe Gamma or managing industrial fleets, Mujina puts the control back where it belongs: in your hands.
